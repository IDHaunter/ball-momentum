# Smiley Skin Update

This update changes only the Smiley skin's appearance and expression.

- Replaced the glossy, glamorized face with a warm vertical gradient: bright yellow at the top, orange through the middle, and a subtle rosy tint at the bottom.
- Added large, round white eyes with dark pupils that move independently, glance around, and blink out of sync.
- Removed eyebrows, blush, teeth, and tongue accents.
- Replaced the open toothy grin with a simple dark curved smile.
- The smile becomes a little wider and flatter as movement speed increases, and briefly changes to a surprised open mouth after impacts and landings.
- The whole face stays inside the existing rotating / squash-stretch ball container.
- Reduced Motion continues to pause the facial animation.
- Soccer, basketball, eye, and alien skins are unchanged. Physics, levels, input, and save keys are unchanged.

The Settings preview was updated to match the new face.
